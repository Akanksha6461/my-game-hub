# Iron Man: Sky Defense

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation or internet required.

- WASD or arrow keys: fly
- Space: fire repulsors
- E: fire the unibeam when fully charged
- P: pause or resume
- Touch: drag on the game to fly and fire automatically; tap the Unibeam button for a charged attack

Destroy drones, collect green cores to repair armor, and protect the skyline. Escaping drones damage your armor. Your best score is saved in the browser when available.

Unofficial fan-made game. All graphics are drawn with Canvas.
